## Have,has,had.

Tui thường đọc thành hev,hez,hed khi đọc nhanh. Mục tiêu là sửa nó. 

Đọc các câu dưới đây từ chậm dần đến nhanh dần. Khi tốc độ chậm mà nói đúng hết mới tăng tốc.

Đến khi nói nhanh luôn đúng thì dừng.

### Vòng 1 — dễ

I have a dog.
She has a cat.
They had a car.
We have a house.
He has a job.

### Vòng 2 — trộn

I have two brothers.
My sister has a new phone.
We had a good time.
They have a big problem.
He had a cold last week.

### Vòng 3 — bắt đầu dài hơn

I have a lot of work today.
She has an interesting idea.
We had a great dinner last night.
They have a meeting this afternoon.
He has a small apartment near the station.
